🎧 Running Playlists

I track my cadence during runs using Spotify playlists organized by BPM. Each playlist targets a specific range to help you find the right pace:

130–140 BPM — Warm-up

Easy warm-up runs and recovery days.
140–150 BPM — Conversational

Comfortable long runs where you can still talk.
150–160 BPM — Tempo

Slightly challenging pace for tempo runs.
165–175 BPM — Race Pace

High-energy playlist for interval sprints & races.
